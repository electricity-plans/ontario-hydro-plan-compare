# Support

Ontario Hydro Plan Compare is a free, volunteer-run hobby project. Help is **best effort**: every message is read,
but a reply can't be promised.

## What we can help with

- **Using the app**: ask in [Discussions → Q&A](../../discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Something that doesn't work**: open a [Bug report](../../issues/new/choose).
- **A utility's file that isn't supported yet**: use "My utility's file doesn't work".

## What we can't help with

- **Whether you should switch plans.** The app gives an estimate from your past usage, not advice.
- **Your bill.** We can't interpret or check individual bills, and can't guarantee any saving.
- **Your account.** For eligibility, billing questions, disputes and when a plan change would start, contact your
  utility. Your utility's bill is always the official amount.

## Please

Never post your bill, your usage file, or any account number, address or meter number. GitHub is public, and search
engines index it.
